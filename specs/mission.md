# Mission

Quran is a free, offline-first app for reading the Quran, keeping the verses
that matter to you, and organizing the verses you use in prayer (du'a) by
topic. It installs from the browser on phones and computers and works without
a connection after the first visit.

## Purpose

Give Muslims a calm, fast, trustworthy place to read and return to the Quran:

- **Read** all 6,236 verses in the Uthmani script with an English translation,
  and pick up where you left off (last read, last recited).
- **Keep** verses in personal collections and prayer topics, ready for daily
  use.
- **Find** any verse by reference (`2:255`) or by searching the Arabic or the
  translation.

The interface borrows the familiar patterns of X.com (feed, lists, explore)
with its own branding (no X logos, names or assets), so it feels obvious on a
phone.

## Primary Audience

**Muslims, as a free public app.** Readers range from those who read Arabic
fluently to those who rely on the English translation, mostly on phones, often
with poor or no connectivity (travel, mosques, commutes).

## Principles

- **Offline-first.** After the first visit everything works without a
  network: text, font, search and the user's lists.
- **Private by design.** No accounts, no backend, no tracking. User data stays
  on the device; backups are files the user controls.
- **Faithful text.** The Arabic is the Tanzil Uthmani text, never edited by
  hand; every source is credited with its license. A text or translation
  without a verified source and license doesn't ship publicly.
- **Respectful and simple.** Surahs are identified by number only. No ads, no
  gamification, nothing that distracts from reading.
- **Accessible.** Keyboard and screen-reader friendly, readable contrast in
  light and dark mode, adjustable Arabic size, an English-only option and a
  distraction-free reading mode.
- **Small, verifiable steps.** Every feature has a spec (requirements, plan,
  validation) and passes its checks before it's merged.

## What Success Looks Like

- A reader can install the app, go offline, and still read, search and use
  their lists without noticing a difference.
- Search finds a verse whether it's typed in everyday or Uthmani spelling,
  with or without diacritics.
- A user's lists and settings survive app updates, and move to another device
  with a backup file.
- Every verse shown can be traced to a credited, licensed source.

## Out of Scope

Tafsir, user accounts, cloud sync, prayer times, sharing, and surah names.
Audio recitation is planned (see [roadmap.md](roadmap.md)).
